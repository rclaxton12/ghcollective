A morning of gratitude: our 2026 Client Appreciation Brunch

Glasshouse CollectiveNovember 18, 20263 min read

On Saturday, October 25, we invited the clients who make our work possible to a fall brunch. It was one of those perfect Arizona mornings, with blue skies, warm sun and just enough of an autumn feel, and a chance for us to say thank you in person.

Why we host it

Most of our business comes from past clients and the friends and family they send our way. A home purchase or sale is a big moment, and many of the people we work with become friends long after closing. This event is our way of celebrating those relationships and spending time together without a contract in sight.

Family portraits with our photographer

The highlight of the morning, and the part many of our clients look forward to every year, is our professional photographer. Guests take turns gathering for family portraits or solo photos against the palms, cactus and desert landscaping, with kids, parents and grandparents all getting in the frame.

With the holidays around the corner, it is an easy way for families to get an updated professional photo without planning a separate session. Watching families laugh their way through their portraits is one of our favorite parts of the day, and clients receive their photos afterward to keep and share.

Brunch and fall touches

Guests arrived to a brunch spread set with fall florals, pumpkins and plenty of pastries and treats. Tables were dressed with sunflowers, burlap and small reminders of the season, and the whole morning had a relaxed, come-as-you-are feel.

Dirty sodas from the Sunday Soda trailer

Guests also enjoyed the Sunday Soda trailer, a restored vintage horse trailer turned mobile soda bar. They served up dirty sodas, one of the biggest drink trends of the past few years.

If you have not tried one yet, a dirty soda starts with a classic fountain soda and gets dressed up with flavored syrups, a splash of cream or coconut cream and often a squeeze of fresh lime. The idea took off in Utah soda shops and spread across the country on social media, and the combinations are endless. Guests of every age lined up to try something new from the menu, and the trailer itself made a great backdrop for photos.

Thank you to our sponsors

We are grateful to our partners at WFG National Title Insurance Company and Elevate Homescriptions, who helped sponsor the refreshments and the day. Events like this are better with good partners, and we appreciate their support of our clients.

Thank you for being part of the collective

To everyone who joined us, thank you for spending the morning with us. And to every client we have had the privilege of working with, whether you could make it or not, we are so grateful for your trust, your referrals and your friendship. We are already looking forward to the next one.
