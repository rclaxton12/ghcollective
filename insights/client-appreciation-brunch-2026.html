A morning of gratitude: our 2026 Client Appreciation Brunch

Glasshouse CollectiveNovember 18, 20262 min read

On Saturday, October 25, we hosted a fall brunch for clients and friends. Blue skies, warm sun and a hint of autumn made it a perfect Arizona morning to say thank you in person.

Family portraits with our photographer

The highlight, and a favorite our guests look forward to each year, was our professional photographer, Stevie Photo Co. Guests gathered for family portraits and solo photos among the palms and cactus, just in time for the holidays.

Brunch and fall touches

The brunch spread was set with fall florals, pumpkins and plenty of pastries, with sunflowers and burlap on the tables and a relaxed, come-as-you-are feel.

Dirty sodas from the Sunday Soda trailer

Guests also enjoyed the Sunday Soda trailer, a restored vintage horse trailer turned mobile soda bar. It served dirty sodas, the drink trend that started in Utah soda shops: classic fountain soda mixed with flavored syrups, a splash of cream and a squeeze of lime. Guests of every age had fun trying something new.

Thank you

Thank you to everyone who joined us, and to all of our clients for your trust, your referrals and your friendship. We are already looking forward to next year.

We are also grateful to our partners at WFG National Title and Elevate Homescriptions for their continued support of our clients and our team.
