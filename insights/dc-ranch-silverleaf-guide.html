What should you know about DC Ranch and Silverleaf in North Scottsdale?

Glasshouse CollectiveSeptember 9, 20265 min read

DC Ranch is a master-planned community in North Scottsdale organized into distinct villages, with Silverleaf as its highest-priced enclave. Across our own 22 closed sales in DC Ranch the median sale price is $2,043,250, and the range runs from $460,000 to $9,050,000. That spread is the most important thing to understand about the community.

How is DC Ranch organized?

DC Ranch is not a single neighborhood. It is a collection of villages, each with its own character, price range and level of gating. Country Club at DC Ranch and Silverleaf are guard-gated. Other villages are not.

Because of that structure, a comparable sale from one village can be a poor guide to value in another, even a few minutes away. Pricing here requires knowing which village a home sits in and which homesites within it carry view corridors.

What makes Silverleaf different?

Silverleaf sits at the top of the DC Ranch price structure. Our Silverleaf closings carry a median of $2,950,000 against $2,043,250 for DC Ranch overall, and Silverleaf accounts for the team's highest recorded sale at $9,050,000.

The community is known for custom homesites in the upper parcels, larger lots, and an architectural review process that shapes what can be built and modified. Buyers planning a renovation should understand that process before they write an offer, not after.

What drives value in this market?

Elevation and view corridor matter enormously. Two homes of similar size and finish can differ substantially in value based on where they sit and what they look at.

Beyond that, buyers weigh lot size and privacy, whether the home is custom or production built, the age of the finishes relative to current expectations, and proximity to community amenities. Square footage alone is a weak predictor of price here.

What should sellers in DC Ranch expect?

Expect a smaller buyer pool and a longer marketing period than in mid-priced Scottsdale. Our Scottsdale sales overall carry a median of 40 days on market, and higher price points typically run longer.

That is not a problem to solve with a price cut on day thirty. It is a reason to price correctly at launch and to market to the specific audience that buys at this level, which is often out of state and often working through an agent rather than browsing listings themselves.

Is DC Ranch a good fit for you?

It suits buyers who want a master-planned setting with community amenities and consistent architectural standards, and who are comfortable with an HOA that actively governs appearance and modification.

Buyers who want an unrestricted lot, a shorter commute to central Phoenix, or a mature-tree lot on flood irrigation will generally be happier in Arcadia or the Biltmore corridor. Both are good answers. They are just different questions.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Silverleaf part of DC Ranch?

Yes. Silverleaf is a guard-gated luxury enclave within the DC Ranch master plan in North Scottsdale, and it sits at the top of the community's price structure.

What is the median home price in DC Ranch?

Across the 22 DC Ranch sales the Glasshouse Collective has closed, the median is $2,043,250 with a range from $460,000 to $9,050,000. Current market medians move over time, so ask for figures on the specific village you are considering.

Does DC Ranch have an HOA?

Yes. DC Ranch has community governance including architectural review, and several villages including Silverleaf are guard-gated. Review the governing documents during your inspection period.
