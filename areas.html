Areas

Where we work.

Built on referrals

Across the Valley, one referral at a time.

Most of our business comes from referrals: past clients, their friends and family, and the people they trust us with. That has taken us into homes all over the Phoenix metro area, from Scottsdale and Paradise Valley to Phoenix, the East Valley and beyond. Here is a look at the areas where we spend the most time.

Scottsdale

Median single-family price around $1.2 million

Scottsdale is known for its resort lifestyle: championship golf, spas and dining, the galleries and nightlife of Old Town, spring training baseball and miles of desert trails in the McDowell Sonoran Preserve. Homes range widely, from condos and townhomes in the south, where prices start closer to $485,000, to custom homes on desert acreage in the north. It draws young professionals, growing families and seasonal residents alike.

Phoenix

Median home price around $450,000

The fifth-largest city in the country and the heart of the Valley, Phoenix offers the widest variety of homes and lifestyles. There are downtown condos close to the arts, sports and dining scene, historic homes with mature trees and character, newer communities to the north, and hillside homes near the city’s mountain preserves. Sky Harbor International Airport makes it one of the easiest places in the Southwest to travel from.

The East Valley

Medians from about $490,000 to the mid-$500,000s

Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler and their neighbors are known for family-friendly communities, highly regarded schools, parks and newer master-planned neighborhoods, along with a strong job base in technology and healthcare. The East Valley offers some of the best value in the Valley for space and amenities, with easy freeway access to Phoenix, Tempe and the airport. Medians run about $490,000 in Mesa and in the mid-$500,000s in Gilbert and Chandler.

Paradise Valley

Median single-family price around $4 million

Tucked between Phoenix and Scottsdale beneath Camelback and Mummy Mountain, Paradise Valley is a quiet residential town known for one-acre minimum lots, privacy, mountain views and celebrated resorts. Most homes are custom estates, and the town does not levy its own property tax. It is the Valley’s most exclusive address, yet only minutes from Scottsdale and Phoenix dining and shopping.

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Glasshouse Collective serve?

We work throughout the greater Phoenix area. Most of our business comes from referrals, so we follow our clients wherever they are buying or selling: most often Scottsdale, Phoenix, Paradise Valley and the East Valley, and regularly the West Valley and surrounding towns as well.

How do home prices compare across the Valley?

As of fall 2026, the median single-family price is roughly $450,000 in Phoenix, about $490,000 in Mesa, the mid-$500,000s in Gilbert and Chandler, around $1.2 million in Scottsdale and around $4 million in Paradise Valley. Medians shift month to month and prices vary widely within each city, so ask us for current figures on the area you are considering.

Do you work outside the areas listed here?

Yes. Because so much of our business comes by referral, we regularly help clients across the entire Phoenix metro area, including the West Valley, the Northeast Valley and nearby towns. If your search crosses several areas, that is a normal part of the work.
