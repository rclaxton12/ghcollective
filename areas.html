The Valley, by the numbers

337 closed sales across 28 cities and towns.

We are not a one-neighborhood team. Since 2008 we have closed nearly $250 million in Valley real estate, from $72,000 condominiums to a $9,050,000 estate. Below are the areas where we transact most often and what we have learned closing business in each one.

Scottsdale

100 closed sales · $747,500 median

Scottsdale is where the team has closed more transactions than anywhere else in the Valley, 100 in total, at a median sale price of $747,500 and a median of 40 days on market. Our Scottsdale sales run from $105,000 condominiums in the south of the city to a $9,050,000 estate in the north, which means we can speak to the entry point and the top of the market with the same authority.

DC Ranch & Silverleaf

22 closed sales · $2,043,250 median

DC Ranch and the Silverleaf community inside it are the deepest part of our luxury practice. We have closed 22 sales here at a median of $2,043,250, including the team’s highest recorded sale at $9,050,000. Guard-gated villages, custom homesites and a distinct architectural review process make this a market where transaction experience matters more than square footage comparisons.

Paradise Valley

$2,910,000 median sale price

Paradise Valley is the Valley’s highest-priced municipality, and our closings here carry a median sale price of $2,910,000. Homes in Paradise Valley sit on larger lots with a one-acre minimum in most of the town, and buyers weigh privacy, mountain views and lot orientation as heavily as the house itself. Expect a longer marketing period here than in surrounding cities.

Arcadia & the Biltmore Corridor

75 closed sales in Phoenix

Arcadia and the Biltmore corridor sit inside the city of Phoenix, where we have closed 75 transactions at a median of $527,500 and a median of 36 days on market. This is a market of irrigated lots, mid-century ranch homes and new construction on teardown sites, often on the same street. Pricing here depends heavily on the block, the lot, and whether flood irrigation is included.

North Scottsdale & McDowell Mountain Ranch

Gated communities and master plans

North Scottsdale covers McDowell Mountain Ranch, Grayhawk, Troon and the master-planned communities along the 101 corridor. Homes here are newer than central Scottsdale, most sit inside an HOA, and community amenities and view corridors drive value as much as interior finish. We represent both buyers relocating into these communities and owners selling out of them.

The East Valley

69 closed sales across Mesa, Chandler and Gilbert

Mesa, Chandler and Gilbert account for 69 of our closings, with medians of $467,450, $348,000 and $433,000 respectively. Mesa moves fastest of the three at a median of 24 days on market. The East Valley is where most of our first-time and move-up buyers transact, and where newer inventory and larger lots are available at prices well below the Scottsdale median.

Area questions

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Glasshouse Collective serve?

Glasshouse Collective serves buyers and sellers throughout the greater Phoenix area. Our 337 closed transactions span 28 Valley cities and towns, led by Scottsdale with 100 sales, Phoenix with 75, Chandler with 26, Mesa with 24 and Gilbert with 19. We also work throughout Paradise Valley, Peoria, Goodyear, Tempe, Queen Creek and Fountain Hills.

Which Scottsdale communities does Glasshouse Collective know best?

DC Ranch and Silverleaf are the communities where we have the deepest transaction history, with 22 closed sales at a median of $2,043,250. We also transact regularly across McDowell Mountain Ranch, Grayhawk, Troon, McCormick Ranch and south Scottsdale.

What is the median home price in Scottsdale versus the East Valley?

Across our own closed sales, the median in Scottsdale is $747,500 while Mesa is $467,450, Gilbert is $433,000 and Chandler is $348,000. Paradise Valley is the highest at a $2,910,000 median. These are our transaction medians rather than market-wide figures, and current market medians change month to month.

Do you work outside the areas listed here?

Yes. The areas on this page are where we transact most often, not a boundary. We have closed sales in 28 Valley cities and towns, and we regularly represent clients moving between them. If your search crosses several areas, that is a normal part of the work.
