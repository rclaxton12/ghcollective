Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who is the Glasshouse Collective?

Glasshouse Collective is a residential real estate team at Compass serving buyers and sellers throughout the Greater Phoenix area. Our collaborative approach combines local market knowledge, thoughtful strategy, marketing expertise and personalized service to help our clients make confident real estate decisions.

What areas does the Glasshouse Collective serve?

Glasshouse Collective serves buyers and sellers throughout the Greater Phoenix area, with extensive experience in Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Arcadia and the East Valley, as well as select West Valley communities. Our knowledge of the Valley helps clients evaluate not only individual homes, but also location, lifestyle, market conditions and long-term value.

Is the Glasshouse Collective part of Compass?

Yes. The Glasshouse Collective is a real estate team brokered by Compass. That affiliation gives clients access to Compass marketing tools and a national and international referral network, paired with the personal service of a local Valley team.

Does the Glasshouse Collective help both buyers and sellers?

Yes. Glasshouse Collective represents both buyers and sellers, and has closed 177 listing-side and 166 buyer-side transactions since 2008. The team guides first-time buyers, move-up buyers, relocating professionals and luxury clients, with sales ranging from $72,000 condominiums to a $9,050,000 Scottsdale estate.

How do I contact the Glasshouse Collective?

You can reach the Glasshouse Collective by phone at (602) 686-6662 or by email at Carolyn@GHCollectiveAZ.com. The team serves clients throughout the Greater Phoenix area seven days a week.
