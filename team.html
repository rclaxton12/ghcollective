Who we are

The Power of Collective Expertise

At Glasshouse Collective, we believe exceptional real estate begins with understanding people. Buying or selling a home is more than a transaction. It is an important life transition, and having the right team beside you can make all the difference.

The word Collective reflects who we are and how we work. Built on a friendship spanning more than three decades, partners Carolyn Glass and Rhonda Claxton created Glasshouse Collective around a shared philosophy: lead with integrity, build lasting relationships, and approach every client with thoughtful strategy and genuine care. Together with Amy, Lori and Kim, our team brings diverse strengths, collaborative problem-solving and a commitment to making the experience as seamless and enjoyable as possible.

Our backgrounds span business, marketing, hospitality, appraisal and new-home development, and those combined talents are what our clients feel most. Together we have closed 337 transactions and nearly $250 million in Valley real estate since 2008. Different perspectives meet on every file, which means better ideas, faster solutions and fewer surprises. With deep roots in marketing and real estate, we also bring a marketer’s perspective to every property we represent. We think beyond simply listing a home, considering how it should be positioned, presented and promoted to reach the right buyers and stand out in the market.

Most importantly, we take the time to listen, communicate openly and understand what matters to you. Whether you are buying your first home, selling a longtime residence, or searching for what comes next, you will have a trusted team of advisors working alongside you every step of the way.

Team questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is Glasshouse Collective?

Glasshouse Collective is a residential real estate team serving buyers and sellers throughout the Greater Phoenix area. Our collaborative approach combines local market knowledge, thoughtful strategy, marketing expertise and personalized service to help our clients make confident real estate decisions.

Who are the agents at Glasshouse Collective?

Glasshouse Collective includes REALTORS® Carolyn Glass, Rhonda Claxton, Amy Brown and Lori Wilson, supported by licensed Transaction Manager Kim Jones. Together, the team brings diverse strengths and a collaborative approach to serving buyers and sellers throughout the Greater Phoenix area.

How much real estate experience does Glasshouse Collective have?

Glasshouse Collective has closed 337 transactions and nearly $250 million in sales volume across the Greater Phoenix area since 2008. Our backgrounds include residential sales, marketing, hospitality, appraisal and new-home development, which gives our clients valuable perspective on property value, positioning, negotiation and the buying and selling process.

How many homes has Glasshouse Collective sold?

Glasshouse Collective has closed 337 transactions representing $249.5 million in sales volume, with individual sale prices ranging from $72,000 to $9,050,000. Those sales span 28 Valley cities and towns, led by Scottsdale, Phoenix, Chandler, Mesa and Gilbert.

What makes Glasshouse Collective different from other real estate teams?

The word Collective reflects how we work. Rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all approach, our team combines individual strengths, market knowledge, marketing expertise and collaborative problem-solving to develop a strategy around each client’s goals. We listen first, communicate openly and remain personally involved from the first conversation through closing.

What areas does Glasshouse Collective serve?

Glasshouse Collective serves buyers and sellers throughout the Greater Phoenix area, with extensive experience in Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Arcadia and the East Valley, as well as select West Valley communities. Our knowledge of the Valley helps clients evaluate not only individual homes, but also location, lifestyle, market conditions and long-term value.
